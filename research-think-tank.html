Research & Think-Tank

Research Collaboration and Think-Tank Initiatives

GIRA convenes institutions, faculty experts, fellows, and policy stakeholders to pursue multidisciplinary research with academic credibility and practical relevance.

Core Areas

A platform for research, policy inquiry, and institutional capacity.

Multidisciplinary Research

Collaborative inquiry across management, economics, public policy, technology, sustainability, education, social development, and related disciplines.

Development Research

Research partnerships with NGOs, development organizations, and institutions working on sustainable development, social innovation, and inclusive growth.

Policy Research

Evidence-based reports, policy briefs, roundtables, and institutional knowledge products for government bodies, international organizations, and public-interest stakeholders.

Institutional Capacity-Building

Research mentoring, publication strategy, doctoral and faculty research workshops, and institutional systems for improving research output.

Global Roundtables

Convenings that bring together academic, policy, industry, and civil-society voices around urgent global challenges.

Management Education Inquiry

Applied research connected to undergraduate and master's management education, international exposure, pedagogy, leadership, and responsible enterprise.

Pathway

From Collaboration to Impact

GIRA supports research partnerships from partner identification through publication, policy translation, and global dissemination.

1

Convene

Identify institutional partners, faculty experts, fellows, and thematic research priorities.

2

Collaborate

Build multidisciplinary teams for research, policy inquiry, publications, and academic programming.

3

Translate

Convert research into reports, white papers, teaching insights, policy briefs, and institutional capacity-building.

4

Amplify

Share outcomes through roundtables, journals, conferences, academic programs, and partner networks.

Research Outputs

Scholarly, institutional, and policy-facing outputs.

Research collaborations may generate academic publications, teaching resources, applied studies, and policy-oriented knowledge products.

Journal articles Case studies Policy briefs White papers Edited volumes Monographs Conference proceedings Institutional reports Teaching notes Impact studies
Think-Tank Function

Evidence-based dialogue for sustainable global impact.

Policy Dialogue

Research projects connected to sustainable development, responsible leadership, innovation, education, and inclusive growth.
