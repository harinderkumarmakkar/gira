Research & Think-Tank

Research Collaboration and Think-Tank Initiatives

GIRA brings institutions, researchers, fellows, and policy stakeholders together for multidisciplinary research with both academic and practical value.

Core Areas

Research, policy work, and support for stronger research teams.

Multidisciplinary Research

Projects can draw on management, economics, public policy, technology, sustainability, education, social development, and related disciplines.

Development Research

GIRA works with NGOs, development organizations, and academic institutions on sustainable development, social innovation, and inclusive growth.

Policy Research

Projects may produce reports, policy briefs, roundtables, and other research for governments, international organizations, and public-interest groups.

Research Capacity

Mentoring, publication planning, and workshops help faculty and doctoral researchers strengthen their work.

Global Roundtables

GIRA brings academic, policy, industry, and civil-society voices together to discuss urgent global issues.

Management Education Inquiry

Research on undergraduate and master's management education, international exposure, teaching, leadership, and responsible enterprise.

Process

How a Research Partnership Develops

GIRA supports partners from the first conversation through planning, research, publication, and policy work.

1

Connect

Bring together institutional partners, faculty experts, fellows, and shared research priorities.

2

Plan

Agree on the research question, roles, methods, and expected outputs.

3

Produce

Develop reports, white papers, teaching materials, policy briefs, and other research outputs.

4

Share

Present the work through roundtables, journals, conferences, academic programs, and partner networks.

Research Outputs

Research for academic, institutional, and policy audiences.

Projects may produce academic publications, teaching resources, applied studies, and policy research.

Journal articles Case studies Policy briefs White papers Edited volumes Monographs Conference proceedings Institutional reports Teaching notes Impact studies
Think-Tank Work

Research discussions with policymakers, industry, and civil society.

GIRA's think-tank work brings academics, policymakers, industry leaders, and civil-society organizations together to discuss research and its practical implications.

Policy Dialogue

Roundtables connect evidence with institutional experience and public-interest priorities.

Applied Research

Research projects connected to sustainable development, responsible leadership, innovation, education, and inclusive growth.

Research Partnership

Discuss research or policy work with GIRA.

Institutions and policy bodies can contact GIRA about multidisciplinary research, workshops, roundtables, or collaborative publications.
