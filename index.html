Management Education

Programs for Undergraduate and Master's Management Education

GIRA supports institutions in designing globally connected management education experiences that extend beyond the classroom through exchange, research, and cross-border academic collaboration.

Faculty Exchange

Short-term and structured academic engagements enabling faculty members to teach, co-research, mentor, deliver masterclasses, and participate in institutional knowledge exchange.

Student Exchange

Institutional pathways for students to experience partner campuses, collaborative learning modules, research exposure, and international academic environments.

International Academic Exposure

Curated international exposure activities focused on management practice, industry visits, policy contexts, cultural learning, and applied research themes.

Semester Exchange Programs

Longer-form institutional mobility opportunities that support credit-bearing or academically integrated semester experiences, subject to partner-institution policies.

GIRA facilitates institutional collaboration frameworks. Program details, eligibility, credit transfer, and participation are governed by the policies of participating institutions.
