What GIRA Does

GIRA supports research, academic exchange, and policy work.

Research Partnerships

Brings faculty, fellows, institutions, and external partners together around research questions that cross disciplines.

Academic Exchange

Supports faculty exchange, student exchange, semester mobility, and other international academic experiences.

International Academic Exposure

Connects management students with institutions, industries, policy settings, and cultural contexts through short academic programs.

Think-Tank Collaboration

Brings research and policy discussions together through roundtables, development studies, and applied research.

Management Education

Programs for Undergraduate and Master's Management Education

GIRA helps institutions design management education experiences that combine classroom study with exchange, research, and cross-border collaboration.

Faculty Exchange

Faculty can teach, conduct research, mentor, lead masterclasses, and exchange ideas with colleagues at partner institutions.

Student Exchange

Students can study at partner campuses, join collaborative modules, and gain research experience in an international academic setting.

International Academic Exposure

Short international programs combine management education with industry visits, policy contexts, cultural learning, and applied research.

Semester Exchange Programs

Partner institutions can develop longer, academically integrated exchanges, including credit-bearing semester programs.

Each program is planned with participating institutions, whose policies determine eligibility, credit transfer, and participation.

Research and Think-Tank Process

How a Research Partnership Develops

GIRA supports partners from the first conversation through research, publication, policy work, and longer-term collaboration.

1

Connect

Bring together institutional partners, faculty experts, fellows, and shared research priorities.

2

Plan

Agree on the research question, roles, methods, and expected outputs.

3

Produce

Develop reports, white papers, teaching materials, policy briefs, and other research outputs.

4

Share

Present the work through roundtables, journals, conferences, academic programs, and partner networks.

Fellowship and Advisory Network
