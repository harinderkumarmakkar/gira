About GIRA

About GIRA

The Global Impact Research Alliance brings institutions together for multidisciplinary research, academic exchange, and policy-relevant work.

Genesis

Research across disciplines, institutions, and regions.

GIRA was founded on the belief that important research often crosses disciplines, institutions, and regions. It brings academic institutions, research centres, think tanks, and expert networks together to work on shared questions.

Its focus is institution-to-institution work: research partnerships, academic exchange, research support, and policy dialogue.

Mission

Support research and academic collaboration across institutions.

GIRA helps institutions build research partnerships, find expert mentors, organise academic exchanges, and produce useful scholarly and policy work.

Vision

A trusted global research alliance.

GIRA aims to be a trusted global partner for interdisciplinary research and management education, helping institutions work together and strengthen their research.

Institutional Model

GIRA collaborates with institutions.

The Alliance works with universities, business schools, research centres, think tanks, NGOs, policy bodies, and international organizations to create sustained research and exchange partnerships.

Core Values

Principles that guide the Alliance.

Integrity

We uphold rigorous, ethical, evidence-based research.

Collaboration

We believe complex problems are better understood when people work across disciplines and borders.

Impact

We support research and academic partnerships that are useful to institutions and the communities they serve.

Inclusivity

We make room for institutions from both established and emerging research communities.

Excellence

We work with scholars, practitioners, and institutions committed to careful, high-quality research.

Respect

We build respectful, collegial relationships while maintaining academic rigour and clear institutional roles.

Advisory Network

Research guidance and shared expertise.

GIRA's advisory network includes academics, industry leaders, policy practitioners, and institutional mentors. They contribute to research, help develop partnerships, and share knowledge across the network.

Advisory and fellowship roles support collaboration, mentorship, and research quality across the network.
