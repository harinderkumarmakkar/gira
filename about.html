About GIRA

About GIRA

The Global Impact Research Alliance is an international platform for institutions seeking to advance multidisciplinary research, global academic exchange, and policy-relevant collaboration.

Genesis

Created for research that crosses disciplines, institutions, and geographies.

GIRA was created from the conviction that consequential research increasingly emerges at the intersection of disciplines, institutions, and geographies. As an initiative of AUA Technologies, GIRA brings together academic institutions, research centres, think tanks, and expert networks to strengthen the global research community and create pathways for collaborative impact.

The Alliance is structured for institutional partnership, research capacity, academic exchange, and practical policy dialogue rather than individual admissions or student-service activity.

Mission

Catalyse meaningful global collaboration.

To catalyse meaningful global collaboration by enabling institutional research partnerships, expert mentorship, academic exchange, and high-relevance scholarly and policy output.

Vision

Build a trusted global alliance.

To become a trusted global alliance for interdisciplinary research and management education partnerships, recognized for connecting institutions, strengthening research ecosystems, and informing sustainable innovation.

Institutional Model

GIRA collaborates with institutions.

The Alliance works with universities, business schools, research centres, think tanks, NGOs, policy bodies, and international organizations to create sustained research and exchange partnerships.

Core Values

Principles that guide the Alliance.

Integrity

We uphold rigorous, ethical, evidence-based research.

Collaboration

We believe complex global problems require collective intelligence across disciplines and borders.

Impact

We prioritize research and academic partnerships that create measurable institutional and societal value.

Inclusivity

We connect emerging and established research ecosystems through equitable institutional collaboration.

Excellence

We recognize and convene scholars, practitioners, and institutions committed to high-quality research output.

Academic Warmth

We build human, respectful partnerships while preserving scholarly restraint and institutional clarity.

Advisory Ecosystem

Mentorship, inquiry, and global knowledge exchange.

GIRA's advisory ecosystem brings together distinguished academics, industry leaders, policy practitioners, and institutional mentors who support rigorous inquiry, partnership development, and global knowledge exchange.

Advisory and fellowship roles are designed to strengthen collaboration, mentorship, and research quality across the institutional network.
