Programs

Global Academic Programs and Exchanges

GIRA works with partner institutions to develop faculty, student, and semester exchanges, as well as research mentorship and collaborative publications for undergraduate and master's management programs.

Exchange Programs

Institutional programs for management education.

Each program is developed directly with participating institutions and reflects their academic policies, expectations, and available capacity.

Faculty Exchange

Faculty exchanges can include guest lectures, teaching modules, joint research, curriculum discussions, executive sessions, and faculty development.

  • Guest teaching modules
  • Masterclasses
  • Joint research workshops
  • Faculty development sessions
  • Co-authored research projects
  • Curriculum and pedagogy dialogue

Student Exchange

Student exchanges give participants access to partner campuses, international classrooms, research communities, and different approaches to management education.

  • Short academic visits
  • Partner-campus exposure
  • Cross-cultural group projects
  • Research-oriented learning modules
  • Institutional learning exchanges

International Academic Exposure

These programs connect management students with partner institutions, industries, public policy settings, social organizations, and local cultures.

  • Global business and strategy
  • Sustainability and development
  • Entrepreneurship and innovation
  • Public policy and governance
  • Digital transformation
  • Responsible leadership

Semester Exchange Programs

GIRA helps institutions plan semester exchanges. Participating institutions decide academic recognition, credit transfer, assessment, and eligibility.

  • Semester mobility pathways
  • Partner-institution coordination
  • Academic recognition discussions
  • Cross-border classroom experience

Institutional Research Mentorship

Senior researchers and fellows work with partner institutions on research design, publication planning, interdisciplinary work, and applied research.

  • Research design mentoring
  • Faculty and doctoral research workshops
  • Publication strategy dialogue
  • Applied inquiry support

Collaborative Publication

GIRA brings researchers together to work on journal articles, edited volumes, monographs, conference papers, white papers, and policy briefs.

  • Journal article collaboration
  • Edited volumes and monographs
  • Conference papers
  • White papers and policy briefs

Each program is planned with participating institutions, whose policies determine eligibility, credit transfer, assessment, and participation.

Program Development

A careful process for academic fit.

GIRA works with each partner to align program goals, participants, academic recognition, research themes, and logistics.

1

Identify

Clarify institutional goals, academic level, discipline focus, and preferred collaboration region.

2

Match

Identify potential institutional partners, faculty expertise, academic settings, and research themes.

3

Design

Shape modules, exchange formats, research outputs, timelines, and academic responsibilities.

4

Coordinate

Coordinate institutional teams while each partner retains responsibility for participation and academic recognition.
